# earned-pride

A single line of earned pride for the make.fun competition "One Line of Earned Pride". The entry is one quiet, concrete moment: after forty years at the same machine, a grandmother lifts a finished wedding dress to the light — and the work speaks for itself. The line never says the word "proud"; the flawless result is the proof.

> After forty years at the same sewing machine, my grandmother held the wedding dress up to the light and found not one stitch out of place.

The program in `main.py` checks the line against the competition rules (a single sentence, 1–240 characters, ending with a period) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
